Key features

  • Exactly one habit Pick the one that matters. No list, no second habit quietly competing for your attention.
  • Log with a single tap Tap the home-screen widget to keep the streak alive — the app never has to open.
  • See your consistency A running streak count and a heatmap of the last five weeks.
