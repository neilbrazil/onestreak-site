What it does

One habit, on purpose

Most habit trackers hand you a list and let you add fifteen things you will abandon by Thursday. OneStreak lets you track exactly one. That constraint is the product, not a limitation.

Log it from the Home Screen

Tap the widget once a day. The streak goes up without the app ever opening — the fastest possible path between deciding to log and having logged.

A chain you can see

A 35-day heatmap shows the run you have built. Seeing an unbroken block of days is the whole psychological trick, and it is on the first screen.

One slip does not wipe the slate

A same-day grace period means missing today does not instantly reset months of progress. You have until the day ends to keep the streak alive.

Rename without losing anything

Made a typo at setup, or want to word the habit differently? Rename it any time and the streak and full history carry over untouched. That is free, not a paid feature.

Your data stays on your device

No account, no sign-in, no sync, no ads. Everything is stored locally on your iPhone and works with the network off.

OneStreak Pro

The free app is the whole core: pick a habit, tap to log, watch the streak, forever. Pro adds three things for people who want more from the history they are building.

  • Streak insights — longest streak and completion rate over time
  • Habit archive — switch to a different habit while every past streak stays saved with its stats
  • Widget styles — change the widget's accent colour

Common questions

Is OneStreak free?
Yes. Picking a habit, logging it from the widget, your streak count and the 35-day heatmap are all free with no time limit and no ads. OneStreak Pro is an optional subscription that adds streak insights, the habit archive and widget styles.
Can I track more than one habit?
No, and that is deliberate. OneStreak tracks exactly one habit. If you want a list of habits there are excellent apps for that — this one is built on the opposite bet, that a single visible streak beats a long list of good intentions.
Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up, no login and no server. Your habit and its history are stored locally on your iPhone.
Can I change my habit later without losing my streak?
You can rename your habit at any time and keep the streak and every logged day — that is free. Switching to a genuinely different habit archives the old one with its full history and starts a new streak from zero; that is part of Pro.
What happens if I miss a day?
There is a same-day grace period: a streak stays alive until the day fully elapses, so you have until the end of the day to log it. Miss a whole day and the streak resets, which is the point of a streak.
Does the widget work without opening the app?
Yes. The whole widget face is a button. Tapping it logs today and updates the streak without launching OneStreak at all.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Everything except the optional subscription purchase works with no network connection.
